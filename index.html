Lawn, pest & pool

YardShelf

Spray, spread and dose by the label. YardShelf is an iPhone app for everything you spray, spread or dose around your home. It starts from what's already on your shelf and works from each product's own label.

  • Add products by barcode, label photo or one photo of the whole shelf
  • Label rates quoted word for word, with a mix calculator for your yard
  • Guides for lawn problems, mosquitoes, ticks, ants and more
  • Soil temperature, spray windows and reminders on a schedule

Functional trainer workouts

Funky Trainer

A workout app for cable functional trainers. Pick your machine and extra attachments, and Funky Trainer shows every exercise you can do with your setup, builds routines for your goal and time, and tracks your progress.

  • Exact pulley position, setup and form cues for your machine
  • Routines built for your goal, split and session length
  • Logs sets with last-time numbers and a suggested weight
  • Progress charts, PRs and CSV export with Pro

Profit Every Stop

Make every stop pay.

Customer profitability and repricing for lawn care and other route-based service companies. It works out what each customer really costs to serve (crew time on site, drive time, truck, materials and overhead), finds the accounts that lose money, and turns that into a repricing plan and ready-to-send letters.

LineItemIQ

Every line, checked.

LineItemIQ audits a company's supplier invoices against its agreements, line by line. It finds overcharges, explains where spend is going, and shows what to negotiate at renewal.

For procurement, finance and operations teams buying recurring supplies and services.
