Most service businesses price by gut feel and look at revenue by customer. Profit Every Stop goes one level deeper and allocates the real cost of serving each job, line by line.

Why it exists

Enterprise pricing discipline, sized for a service business.

Profit Every Stop is built by a pricing professional with more than 20 years of experience, including leading pricing for a distribution business. There, he built a line-level profitability model that costed every order line for product, shipping, commissions, warehousing and handling, showing the true profit on each customer and product.

Large companies pay consultants a lot of money for that kind of visibility. Route-based service businesses face the same questions — which customers, which services, which routes actually make money — with far less help. Profit Every Stop brings the same approach to them.

The goal isn't more reports. It's knowing, stop by stop, where the money is made — and where it quietly leaks away.

Questions

Do I need to use Jobber?

The first version connects to Jobber. If you run your business on a different system and are interested, request early access and say which one — it helps decide what comes next.

How is this different from Jobber's own reports?

Job-management tools report revenue and job counts well. Profit Every Stop allocates your real costs — drive time, labor, materials, equipment and overhead — to each customer and job, so you see profit rather than just sales.

What happens to my data?

Your data is used only to produce your own reports. It isn't sold or shared. See the privacy policy for details.
