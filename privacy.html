Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy describes how Toolshed Apps LLC ("we", "us") handles information in our iPhone apps, including YardShelf and Funky Trainer, and on toolshedapps.com.

What we collect

  • Information you enter in the app — such as yard tasks, products, workout routines and workout history. This is stored so the app can work.
  • Location — YardShelf may use your location, only with your permission, to look up local weather and soil conditions. You can enter a location manually instead.
  • Account information — if an app offers sign-in or sync, we store the email address or sign-in identifier needed to keep your data attached to your account.
  • Purchase information — in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, not your payment details.
  • Diagnostics — basic crash and performance data, as provided by Apple, to help us fix bugs.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show third-party ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We don't share your personal information with anyone except the service providers needed to run the apps (for example, cloud hosting for sync), and only for that purpose.

Where your data lives

Data you enter is stored on your device. If an app supports sync or backup, it is also stored with our hosting provider so it can be restored on your other devices. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time by deleting the app from your device. If you have an account with us and want it and its data removed from our servers, email support@toolshedapps.com and we'll take care of it.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted in the app.
